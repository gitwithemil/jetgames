# BACKROOMS / SAHUR

A browser-based first-person 3D survival horror game. Explore Level 00, recover five power fuses, and escape through the green exit while Tung Tung Tung Sahur searches for you.

## Run locally

From this directory, run `python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory dist`, then open http://localhost:8080. The game uses bundled Three.js and fonts and requires a browser with WebGL. All gameplay works offline once served locally.

## Publish on GitHub Pages

The `Deploy to GitHub Pages` workflow publishes `dist` when changes are pushed to `main` or when the workflow is run manually. Add this project to a public GitHub repository, then set **Settings → Pages → Build and deployment → Source** to **GitHub Actions**. The workflow run will provide the public site URL.

## Controls

WASD / arrows: move. Mouse: look. Shift: sprint. C: crouch. E: recover fuses / exit. F: flashlight. M: explored map. Escape: pause. Touch devices have a joystick, drag-to-look, action buttons, and a crouch toggle. Use **Save and return to menu** from pause to suspend an expedition on this device.

## Features

Detailed procedural wallpaper, carpet and ceiling textures; branching rooms, light flicker and props; stage-specific objectives and enemy senses; flashlight with shadows and finite charge; adjustable screen exposure, flashlight output and beam width; stamina and crouching; an explored map; procedural ambience and positional threat cues; difficulty and optional challenge settings; accessibility and configurable mobile controls; restart, victory and loss screens.

All environment textures, enemy geometry and sounds are generated locally. Three.js is distributed under its MIT license in `dist/vendor/LICENSE.three.txt`. Barlow Condensed, DM Sans and IBM Plex Mono are bundled with their SIL Open Font Licenses in `dist/fonts/`.

## Four-stage campaign (v2.0)

- Level 00 / The Lobby: five fuses, Sahur tracks sight and sound.
- Level 01 / The Poolrooms: three timed circulation relays, cyan tiles, animated water ripples and pool ladders. The Smiler tracks flashlight use.
- Level 02 / The Pipeworks: four timed pressure valves, pipes, pressure gauges and floor grates. The blind Hound tracks loud movement and control operation.
- Level 03 / The Red Archive: three signal tapes, red lighting and instanced book collections. The Watcher freezes when observed with the flashlight; Sahur patrols simultaneously.

All four stages have different connected layouts. Each exit unlocks a checkpoint on this device. Level cards allow starting at any stage. Q throws a nine-second noise decoy; R scans the nearest objective with a twelve-second cooldown. Supply boxes restore battery, stamina and one decoy. Objectives and supplies award points; completing a stage adds a time bonus. Failed-stage points reset on retry. The best completed score is stored locally. Hold E or the touch E button to operate relays and valves; releasing or moving cancels the work.

Static box props are instanced by material to limit rendering overhead. Old environments dispose their geometry, textures and materials when switching levels. Enemy models persist across stages. Distinct entity cues and floor-dependent footsteps are generated by Web Audio.

Validation: all objective/supply/exit route segments checked with player collision radius; four unique maps; enemy sensory rules; timed work; actual exit lock and transition; campaign completion; Watcher gaze freeze in gameplay; decoy investigation; scare pause/retry/settings lifecycle. Tests use actual Three.js geometry with a stub renderer. Browser GPU rendering and audio playback remain unverified in this environment.

Fatal attacks have four enemy-specific pose and sound profiles; Sahur swings his bat and opens his grin, the Hound leaps, the Smiler looms and the Watcher appears as a tall close-up. ## Brainrot roster and rare stun weapon (v3.0)

Added original procedural character interpretations:

- Ballerina Cappuccina: cup head, coffee/foam rim, pink tutu and pointe shoes; slows while observed in the flashlight beam.
- Brri Brri Bicus Dicus Bombicus: dark bird, long beak, gold gladiator armour, red crest/cape and raspberry necklace; charges in bursts.
- Spioniro Golubiro: pigeon, trenchcoat, fedora, glasses and camera; alerts nearby unstunned entities after detecting the player.
- Bombardino Crocodilo: crocodile head, aircraft body, wings, engines and spinning propellers; patrols by sight.

The campaign has two threats per stage and three in the Red Archive. Character animation, sight/sound rules, decoy reactions, audio cues and close-up framing are integrated with the existing enemy system. Reference descriptions consulted: https://plants-vs-brainrots.fandom.com/wiki/Brri_Brri_Bicus_Dicus_Bombicus ; https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Spioniro_Golubiro ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_brainrot . No third-party model or sound assets were downloaded.

The optional stun projector requires three components in distant research cases (2.8 seconds of stationary extraction each), followed by four seconds of assembly at the bench near the starting corridor. Extraction and firing make noise. The projector has three charges, a 12 m aimed range, a four-second cooldown and an eight-second nonlethal stun. Misses spend charges. Enemies cannot move, sense, alert companions or attack during a stun. A faint blue ring effect marks the stun. Charges can be restored by another full component hunt and assembly; ordinary supply boxes do not replenish them. Cases can only be looted once per stage.

Use Space, left-click while pointer-locked, or the on-screen STUN button. Aim at the body; walls and colliding props block the pulse. Weapon ownership and remaining charges carry into the next stage. Retrying restores the loadout at the start of that stage, so a weapon acquired during a failed attempt must be earned again. Starting a new expedition resets the weapon.

## Tactical lighting (v3.1)

B cycles three beam modes: Focus (24 m, balanced battery use), Flood (14 m, wide and economical) and Boost (30 m, narrow and brighter, faster drain). A procedural projection texture adds a central hotspot and soft falloff. Battery voltage dims output smoothly below 20%; low and critical reserve warnings replace repetitive low-battery flicker. Boost overheats after ten seconds of continuous use and automatically returns to Focus; it cannot be selected again until heat falls to 30%.

Hold V or the WIND control while still to hand-crank an emergency reserve up to 25%. This drains stamina, produces an audible mechanism sound and attracts sound hunters. The lamp is suppressed while winding. Reaching the cap latches the input until release, avoiding flicker or an infinite battery exploit. If the lamp had switched off on depletion, F switches it back on after recharging. Cranking prevents simultaneous stun firing. Pause freezes battery use and heat.

Enemy illumination uses beam origin, direction, angle, range, voltage and wall/prop line of sight. Watcher freezing and Ballerina slowing require a beam hit. Smiler and Spioniro detection range depends on beam mode's light signature. The beam switches off while cranking or during a blackout reveal.

Walls, room partitions, ceilings and batched static props now cast flashlight shadows in High quality; floor and wall surfaces receive them. Low quality retains its shadow-free performance setting, but enemy beam occlusion remains checked in both quality modes. Ambient darkness is retained.

Validation: mode drain ordering, overheating/cooling, directed beam and occlusion, pause, emergency reserve cap, crank latching, shadow-caster configuration and the existing full gameplay regression suite. GPU rendering, shadow appearance and audible playback remain unverified in this environment.

Select a charted corridor to pin a waypoint. Routes use only charted, collision-clear passages, include a distance estimate, and avoid furniture at the player's actual position. Select the same point again or use Clear waypoint to remove it. Keyboard users can move the survey cursor with arrows, pin with Enter, and clear with Delete. The optional Supply Shortage challenge begins each stage with 65% flashlight battery and one decoy, and awards 25% more points; standard runs are unchanged. The HUD calls out low or exhausted stamina and exposes live meter values to assistive technology.

## Stealth, replay and extraction (v3.4)

Sight-based entities have forward patrol vision (130°) and wider pursuit vision (200°); hearing and close contact still apply. Watcher freezing still uses the actual unobstructed flashlight beam. Entity sound buses independently follow each actor relative to the camera, with distance attenuation and low-pass wall muffling. Captions remain available when muted.

New expeditions randomize objectives, supplies and enemy starting positions with a persistent seed. Room layouts stay recognizable. Retry and resume preserve placements; older seedless saves retain the original layout. Local Lobby circuits restore by recovered fuse identity.

After holding E at a relay or valve, an accessible, paused terminal opens: Poolrooms require branch routing; Pipeworks require equal pressure and the requested total. Cancelling leaves the machine unfinished. Archive tapes encode a three-digit exit signal with a documented +10 offset. Decode the exit terminal, then hold E at the door for eight noisy seconds to transmit and escape. Leaving, releasing E or pausing cancels extraction; the decoded signal persists in saves. The ending resolves all four environments.

The HUD hides idle heat, the hand-crank above 25% battery, early control hints after 45 seconds, and inactive weapon details. Touch and keyboard controls remain available.
